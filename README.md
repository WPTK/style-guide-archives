# Style Guide Archives

A public archive of style guides from around the world: brand identity manuals and graphic standards, editorial style manuals, technical-writing guides, and software UI guidelines. They come from companies, governments and institutions that still exist and from ones that don't, such as the Bell System, NASA and the New York City Transit Authority.

## Where the guides are

The archive holds 976 files (975 PDFs and one EPUB, 11.75 GB). That is too big for one GitHub repository, so the guides are split across six repositories by category. This repository holds the catalog; the guides are in these:

| Repository | What it holds | Guides | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| [style-guide-archives-brand-identity](https://github.com/WPTK/style-guide-archives-brand-identity) | Corporate identity manuals and logo standards | 119 | 1.92 GB |
| [style-guide-archives-editorial](https://github.com/WPTK/style-guide-archives-editorial) | Style manuals for writers, editors and printers | 216 | 1.87 GB |
| [style-guide-archives-tech-and-software](https://github.com/WPTK/style-guide-archives-tech-and-software) | Technical-writing guides and software UI guidelines | 169 | 1.68 GB |
| [style-guide-archives-government-a-g](https://github.com/WPTK/style-guide-archives-government-a-g) | Government, transit, wayfinding, Olympic and institutional graphic standards, organisations A to G | 167 | 2.05 GB |
| [style-guide-archives-government-h-r](https://github.com/WPTK/style-guide-archives-government-h-r) | The same, organisations H to R | 162 | 2.00 GB |
| [style-guide-archives-government-s-y](https://github.com/WPTK/style-guide-archives-government-s-y) | The same, organisations S to Y | 143 | 2.22 GB |

Inside each repository, files are named `<category>/<organization>/<year>-<title>.pdf`, and an `INDEX.md` lists every file with its year, size and the page it came from. The government repositories are split on the first letter of the organisation's folder name.

## The catalog

[`CATALOG.md`](CATALOG.md) lists where each guide lives online, including the many that are not archived yet. Each row has a rights note and a link check. Sections 7 and 8 list the guides still wanted and the gaps in coverage.

## Large files

Files of 100 MB or more are stored with [Git LFS](https://git-lfs.com). Run `git lfs install` before you clone, or those files will arrive as small pointer files instead of PDFs.

## Rights

These are copies of documents published elsewhere. Copyright stays with the original owners. Many are US government works or US publications from before 1931, which are in the public domain in the United States. The rest were published free by their owners or are third-party scans, and they are here for reference and research. `CATALOG.md` marks the rights status of each one.

If you own the rights to something here and want it removed, open an issue naming the file.

Know of a guide that is missing? Open an issue with a link.
